National Flag

The National Flag is a horizontal ticolour of india saffron (kesaria) at the top, while in the middle and India green at the bottom in equal proportion.

National Anthem

The song Jana-gana-mana, composed originally in Bangla by Rabindranath Tagore, was adopted in its Hindi version by the Contituent Assemble as the National Anthem of India on January 24, 1950`

National Song

The song Vande Mataram, composed in Sanskrit by Bankimchandra Chatterij, was a source of inspiation to the people in their stuggle for freedom. It has an equal status with Jana-gana-mana

National Bird

The Indian Peacock, Pavo cristatus, the national bird of India, is a colourful, swan-sized bird, with a fan-shaped crest of feathers, a while patch under the eye and a long, slender neck.
